Lion Caucus and Advocate Penn State hosted their annual Fall Advocacy Festival to support federal student aid programs that benefit nearly 20,000 Penn State students each year. Penn Staters can take action by emailing Pennsylvania's U.S. senators and representatives to advocate for continued funding.
