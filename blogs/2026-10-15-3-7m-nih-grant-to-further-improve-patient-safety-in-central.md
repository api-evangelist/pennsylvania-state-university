Penn State researchers have spent more than a decade making central line placement safer for patients. Now, a $3.7 million, five-year grant from the National Institutes of Health's National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute will allow the researchers to expand their system and further reduce complications, infections and other injuries for patients receiving central lines.
