Student filmmaker Ben Stanton worked to improve a strong project from class and make it festival-worthy — boosting his confidence and making connections in the on-campus film community as a result. Stanton conceived and produced “Sapphire,” a film focusing on Penn State Blue Band feature twirler Mackenzie Bronk and her final performance at Beaver Stadium last fall.
