Following the announcement of 11 Road Map workstreams in September, University leaders, in collaboration with workstream sponsors and leads, have solidified preliminary workstream targets. The preliminary targets establish initial goals to guide planning and inform recommendations for advancing key University priorities. As planning continues, Penn State faculty, staff and other community members are urged to get involved and share their expertise, experience and ideas to help shape the workstreams’ plans and recommendations.
