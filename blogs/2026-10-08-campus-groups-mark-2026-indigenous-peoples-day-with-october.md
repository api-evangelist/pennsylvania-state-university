The Indigenous Peoples' Student Association, in coordination with the Paul Robeson Cultural Center, the Center for the Performing Arts, University Libraries and Educational Equity, is offering events at Penn State's University Park campus in recognition of Indigenous Peoples Day 2026, to be held this year on Monday, Oct. 12.
